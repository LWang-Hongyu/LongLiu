4. Structural Violation Requirement (V5 design)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem: CRUX violation is transient (epoch 7-8 only), then recovers due to NCCL state artifact.
This makes comparison threshold-sensitive ('绊了一下' vs '走错了路').

Solution: Increase comm duty cycle so steady-state slowdown > c_i threshold even after noise.

V4 duty cycle: ~0.70 (512MB, 30ms compute)
  → overlap = 0.49, expected contention slowdown ≈ 1.5x
  → with c_i=1.6 (tight), threshold at 1.6x → violation is marginal & transient

V5 target: 1GB payload, 30ms compute
  → estimated duty cycle ≈ 0.90, overlap ≈ 0.81, expected slowdown ≈ 1.8x
  → with c_i=1.7, steady-state slowdown 1.8x > 1.7x → STRUCTURAL violation
  → LongLiu tight job gets ~2:1 bandwidth → slowdown ~0.9x < 1.7x → always met
